A note from your guide

More than a trek — a journey into the heart of the mountains.

For me, guiding isn't a job. It's the chance to share the trails, villages, and stories I grew up with.

Since 2017 we've led travellers through the awe-inspiring landscapes of the Himalaya — from quiet rhododendron forests to the high passes above 5,000 m. Every itinerary is shaped around the people who live along the trail, so each day is as much about the chai and the conversation as it is the view.

We work with local Nepali guides from across the Himalayan regions. Hiring locally keeps your trip authentic and makes sure your money supports the communities that make these mountains feel like home.
